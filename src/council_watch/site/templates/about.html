About Council Watch Austin

Council Watch Austin tracks Austin City Council activity and publishes plain-language coverage of agendas, votes, and decisions. Our goal is to make it easier for residents to understand what their council is doing.

What We Track

  • Every City Council and committee meeting
  • How each council member votes on every item
  • Agenda items including resolutions, ordinances, and contracts
  • Historical patterns in voting behavior

Methodology

Data is fetched daily via public APIs and stored as structured JSON. The site is statically generated from that data — no database, no dynamic server. Source code and raw data are available on GitHub.

Coverage Philosophy

We don't compete on breaking news. Our focus is on patterns: who votes together, what kinds of items pass unanimously versus contentiously, and where the gaps are between what council members say and how they vote.
